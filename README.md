## 📱 Projects

### Simplin

A visual schedule app designed to support the daily routines of people with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD).

The app helps users organize their activities through visual resources, making routines more structured and predictable.

Accessibility was an important part of the project, with features such as **Dynamic Type** and **VoiceOver**.

**Technologies:** SwiftUI, Core Data, Local Notifications

Together with colleagues from the Apple Developer Academy | Mackenzie, I co-authored a scientific paper about the project and its accessibility aspects. The paper was accepted for **LACLO 2024**.

I worked as both a **developer and Product Lead**, helping define priorities, organize deliveries, and guide the team throughout development.

**Technologies:** SwiftUI, SpriteKit

### Pashion

A fashion design app created for fashion students and beginners.

Pashion allows users to create collections, design garments, organize references, and generate technical specification sheets.

The project combines drawing tools, moodboards, collections, and document generation to support the creative process from ideation to garment development.

**Technologies:** Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, Core Data

Pashion was developed as my **Apple Developer Academy graduation project** and was published on the App Store in 2024.

### Sapear

A gamified app designed to support the auditory habilitation process of children after cochlear implantation.

The experience is organized into different stages, covering sound detection, discrimination, recognition, and comprehension.

I contributed to the development of new phases and to the redesign of the experience.

**Technologies:** Swift, AVFoundation

### Game of Life

An implementation of **Conway's Game of Life**, exploring how complex patterns can emerge from a simple set of rules.

The project simulates generations of cells on a two-dimensional grid, following the classic rules of survival, death, and reproduction.
